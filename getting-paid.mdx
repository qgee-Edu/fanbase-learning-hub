## Lesson 1: The three ways to earn on Fanbase

Every dollar you earn on Fanbase comes from one of three sources.

<Frame caption="The three ways to earn: Loves, Subscriptions, and Direct Content Revenue">
  <img src="/images/business-center/1-1-three-ways-to-earn.svg" alt="Three ways to earn on Fanbase: Loves, Subscriptions, Direct Content" />
</Frame>

### 1.1 Loves (Fan Tips)

Loves are Fanbase's virtual currency. Fans purchase Love packs and send them to you as tips on any post, during a live stream, in an Audio Room, or as a direct reaction to your content.

**How the math works:**

- 1 Love = $0.005 (half a penny)
- Fans can tip you any amount, from 1 Love up to 25,000 at once
- Loves appear in your Revenue Dashboard under the **Loves** category

<Tip>
**Example:** A fan sends you 1,000 Loves during a live stream. That's $5.00 added to your earnings. If 10 fans each send 1,000 Loves during that stream, that's $50.00.
</Tip>

<Note>
**Coming soon:** An interactive Loves-to-dollars calculator will live here so you can play with different tip and audience scenarios.
</Note>

### 1.2 Subscriptions

Fans can subscribe to your Fanbase page for a monthly fee. You set the price from Fanbase's price tiers ($0.99 to $49.99/month). Subscribers get access to all your exclusive content for as long as they're subscribed.

- You choose your subscription price. Fanbase does not set it for you.
- **Subscription Price Selection** lets you offer multiple price options for fans to choose from.
- Subscription revenue appears in your dashboard monthly as fans' billing cycles renew.
- Exclusive content is automatically unlocked for any active subscriber.

<Warning>
**Verify with platform team:** Confirm current Fanbase fee/revenue share on subscriptions. Confirm whether there is a transaction fee on individual Love purchases.
</Warning>

### 1.3 Direct Content Revenue

In addition to tips and subscriptions, creators earn when fans purchase or unlock content through Love-denominated actions.

<Info>
**Important platform update:** Individual post paywalls have been removed. Exclusive content now requires a full subscription. Fans can no longer unlock a single post.
</Info>

<Warning>
**Verify with Justin/Isaac:** Confirm whether any individual content unlock still exists in the current model, or if all exclusive access requires subscription only.
</Warning>

## Lesson 2: How payments flow

Here's what happens behind the scenes when a fan pays you.

<Frame caption="From fan to creator in 5 steps: pay, process, record, accumulate, cash out">
  <img src="/images/business-center/1-1-payment-flow.svg" alt="Payment flow from fan to creator in five steps" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fan pays">
    Fan purchases Loves or subscribes to your page via the Fanbase app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stripe processes the transaction">
    Fanbase processes the transaction through Stripe, the platform's payment processor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revenue is recorded">
    Earnings are recorded in real time and appear in your Revenue Dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Balance accumulates">
    Your balance builds. You can cash out anytime once you hit the $5.00 minimum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cashout to PayPal">
    Cashout is processed via PayPal to your connected account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**Image idea for Andy:** A horizontal carousel that lets a reader tap through each of these 5 steps with an animated micro-illustration for each one would land really well here.
</Tip>

**Key facts:**

- **Minimum cashout:** $5.00
- **Payout method:** PayPal (must be connected in your payment account setup, see [Module 1.2](/business-center/payment-account-setup))
- **Payout processing time:** Verify with Lauren/Justin for current timeline

<Warning>
**Verify:** Confirm current cashout processing time. Confirm whether international creators have different payout options or requirements.
</Warning>

## Lesson 3: Reading your Revenue Dashboard

Your Revenue Dashboard is your financial command center on Fanbase. It shows everything: total balance, earnings by type, and full transaction history.

### How to access your Revenue Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile">
    Tap your **Profile** icon (bottom right of the navigation bar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap the **Settings/gear** icon (top right of your profile page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Revenue Dashboard">
    Choose **Revenue Dashboard** from the menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What you'll see

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Total Balance" icon="wallet">
    Your current available earnings. This is the amount you can cash out right now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Loves" icon="heart">
    Total earnings from fan tips across all of your content and live sessions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Subscriptions" icon="repeat">
    Your monthly recurring revenue from active subscribers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transaction History" icon="list">
    A chronological log of every Love tip, subscription payment, and cashout. You can filter by date range and payment type.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
**What to watch:** Your Loves-to-Subscriptions ratio tells you a lot about your audience. A high Loves number with low subscriptions usually means fans are engaged but haven't committed. That's your signal to create more exclusive content and make a clear 'subscribe' ask.
</Tip>
