## Lesson 1: How subscriptions work on Fanbase

On Fanbase, a subscription is a monthly recurring relationship between a fan and a creator. Once a fan subscribes:

- They get access to **all** of your exclusive content, past and future
- They keep that access for as long as they remain subscribed
- They're billed automatically each month until they cancel
- Their payment flows into your Revenue Dashboard the same way Loves do

<Frame caption="Public content drives discovery. Exclusive content drives revenue.">
  <img src="/images/business-center/1-3-public-vs-exclusive.svg" alt="Public vs Exclusive content comparison" />
</Frame>

### The 'no individual paywall' rule

Fanbase removed individual content paywalls. That means a fan can't pay Loves to unlock one post. If they want access to your exclusive content, they subscribe.

<Info>
**Why this matters for you:** It simplifies your strategy. You don't have to price every post. You price one thing (your monthly subscription) and everything exclusive sits behind it.
</Info>

<Warning>
**Verify:** Confirm whether any individual unlock options still exist for legacy content or specific creator tiers.
</Warning>

## Lesson 2: Choosing your subscription price

You set your own price within Fanbase's allowed range. The platform doesn't pick for you.

The current allowed subscription price tiers are: **$0.99, $1.99, $2.99, $4.99, $9.99, $14.99, $19.99, $24.99, and $49.99 per month.**

### The pricing question creators actually ask

The question isn't 'what's the highest price I can charge.' It's 'what's the price a real fan will pay every month for what I make.'

A useful way to think about it:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Entry pricing" icon="seedling">
    **$0.99 to $2.99/mo.** Lower commitment. Best when your audience is large but newer, or when your exclusive content is mostly bonus posts and behind-the-scenes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Standard pricing" icon="tree">
    **$4.99 to $9.99/mo.** The sweet spot for most creators. Strong perceived value for a real content stream: weekly exclusive posts, regular drops, member-only Lives.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Premium pricing" icon="crown">
    **$14.99 and up.** Best when you deliver structured value: a course, a coaching cohort, a tight-knit community, or content fans literally can't get elsewhere.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Three questions before you set a price

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What do subscribers actually get?" icon="gift">
    List it out. Number of exclusive posts per month, member-only Lives, DM access, early access. The clearer the list, the easier it is for you to price and for fans to buy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often will I deliver?" icon="calendar">
    Subscription is a promise. If you can post one exclusive piece a week, price for that. Don't price for what you wish you'd deliver.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the comparable in my niche?" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Look at three creators in your space who you respect. What are they charging? Don't undercut to win. Don't overcharge to look premium. Anchor.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Lesson 3: Setting your subscription price in the app

<Frame caption="The Set Subscription Price screen with all nine price tiers and an optional description field">
  <img src="/images/business-center/1-3-price-selector.png" alt="Set Subscription Price screen showing all price tier options" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile">
    Tap your **Profile** icon (bottom right).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap the **Settings/gear** icon (top right).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Subscription settings">
    Look for **Subscription** or **Subscription Pricing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your price">
    Pick a price from the allowed range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="(Optional) Enable Subscription Price Selection">
    Turn on **Subscription Price Selection** to offer multiple price points so fans can choose what works for them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save your changes. The new price applies to new subscribers immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Verify in-app:** Confirm the exact path to subscription settings, and the exact label of 'Subscription Price Selection' in the current build.
</Warning>

### Subscription Price Selection: why offer multiple tiers?

Offering 2 to 3 price points lets a fan self-select into the level of support that matches their budget.

<Info>
**Example structure:**
- **$4.99/mo** Supporter (same content, lower price, lower commitment)
- **$9.99/mo** Member (same content, standard price)
- **$19.99/mo** Super Fan (same content, plus DM access or shoutouts, if you offer that)

Each tier unlocks the same exclusive content. The difference is the level of access or perks you choose to layer on at higher prices.
</Info>

## Lesson 4: Pricing strategy that actually works

### Start lower than your gut says

Most new creators price too high because they're pricing the work they put in, not the value the fan is getting. Start at the floor of your range, prove the value, then raise the price for new subscribers later.

### Make the 'why subscribe' obvious

Your subscription will not sell itself just because you set a price. Every public post is a chance to remind fans what they get when they subscribe.

### Bundle around moments

Premieres. Watch Parties. Live Audio sessions. The strongest 'subscribe now' moments are when something is happening, not when nothing is.

<Tip>
**A good rhythm:** Public content drives discovery, Stories drive frequency, exclusive content earns the subscription, Lives and Audio Rooms drive the *subscribe now* moment.
</Tip>
