## Lesson 1 -- Welcome to Fanbase

### What Is Fanbase?

Fanbase is a creator-first social platform built around one idea: your audience should be able to support you directly, and you should be able to earn from day one.

Unlike most social platforms where monetization is locked behind follower thresholds, ad revenue splits, or invite-only programs, Fanbase gives every creator access to earning tools from the moment they sign up. There's no minimum follower count. No waiting list. No algorithm gatekeeping who gets to make money.

Here's the simplest way to think about it:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Other platforms" icon="xmark" iconType="solid" color="#ef4444">
    You build an audience, and then hope the platform eventually lets you monetize.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fanbase" icon="check" iconType="solid" color="#5B3FD4">
    You build an audience and monetize at the same time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### How Fanbase Is Different

Every platform has a model. Here's how Fanbase compares to what you might already know:

| Platform | Monetization Access | Revenue Model | Direct Fan Payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 10,000+ followers or Meta invite-only | Brand deals, affiliate links, Live badges | No |
| YouTube | 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours to apply | YouTube keeps 45% of ad revenue | No |
| Substack | Open to all, but text/newsletter only | Subscription fees | Yes, but limited formats |
| Snapchat | Spotlight rewards are algorithmic and unpredictable | Platform-controlled | No |
| **Fanbase** | **Every creator, from day one** | **Loves + subscriptions, you keep the majority** | **Yes** |

### Who Is Fanbase For?

Fanbase works for creators at any stage, but it's especially built for:

- Musicians, artists, and performers who want to monetize their craft directly
- Educators and coaches who want to offer exclusive content to their community
- Fitness creators, lifestyle creators, and niche content makers with a loyal (even if small) audience
- Creators already on other platforms who want to own a direct revenue stream
- New creators who want to start earning without waiting for a platform to approve them

<Tip>
  You don't need to be verified. You don't need 10K followers. You need a Fanbase account and a story worth telling.
</Tip>

## Lesson 2 -- Finding Your Way Around

### The Five Things You'll Use Every Day

When you open Fanbase, you'll see a bottom navigation bar with five icons:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Home" icon="house">
    Your main feed: content from creators you follow, recommended posts, Flickz, and Stories. This is where you discover what's happening on the platform.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Search" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Find creators, content, Audio Rooms, and Camps. Use this to explore what's trending, find your niche community, or search for specific people.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Create (the + button)" icon="plus">
    This is your starting point for everything you make. Tap it to choose from: Post, Story, Live, Flickz, Audio, or Plus. Every piece of content you publish starts here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notifications" icon="bell">
    Loves, comments, new subscribers, follows, and activity on your content all show up here. Check this regularly -- it tells you what's resonating.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Profile" icon="user">
    Your public creator page plus access to your settings, revenue dashboard, and account management. This is where your audience sees everything you've posted and can subscribe to you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Your Content Creation Menu

When you tap the **+** button, a creation menu appears with six options:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Post" icon="image">
    Share a photo or video to your feed. Posts stay on your profile permanently unless you delete them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Story" icon="clock">
    Share a photo or video that disappears after 24 hours. Great for behind-the-scenes moments, quick updates, and fan interaction.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live" icon="circle-dot" color="#ef4444">
    Go live with your audience in real time. Fans can send Loves, leave comments, and interact with you during the broadcast.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Flickz" icon="film">
    Fanbase's short-form video format. Record or upload videos up to 2 minutes long with built-in editing tools, filters, music, and effects.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio" icon="microphone">
    Start or join an Audio Room. Host live audio conversations, Q&As, or community discussions. Fans can listen, interact, and send Loves in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Plus (FB+)" icon="play">
    Upload long-form, high-quality video content from your phone or desktop. Think of this as your channel: full-length videos, tutorials, series episodes, and more.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Lesson 3 -- Setting Up Your Profile

### Why Your Profile Matters

On Fanbase, your profile is your storefront. When someone discovers you, they land on your profile page and are deciding:

- Is this creator worth following?
- Do I want to subscribe?
- Does what they make align with what I care about?

A complete profile answers all three questions before the visitor has to ask. An incomplete one leaves money on the table.

### How to Edit Your Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile">
    Tap the profile icon (bottom right of the navigation bar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Account Settings">
    Tap the settings/gear icon in the top right corner of your profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Edit Profile">
    You'll see the full Edit Profile screen with all your customizable fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in every field">
    See the field guide below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Tap Save or the checkmark before leaving the screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Your Profile Fields -- and Why Each One Matters

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Profile Photo" icon="camera">
    The image that appears next to your name everywhere on Fanbase -- in feeds, search results, Audio Rooms, and Live. Use a clear, high-quality image of your face or brand logo.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cover Image" icon="panorama">
    The banner at the top of your profile page. Use this to communicate your niche, vibe, or a current campaign.

    <Warning>Confirm recommended cover image dimensions with the Fanbase team before publishing.</Warning>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Username" icon="at">
    Your @handle on Fanbase. Choose something simple, memorable, and consistent with your other social handles if possible.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bio" icon="pen">
    Your bio is your pitch. Tell visitors who you are, what you make, and why they should stick around. Keep it to two or three sentences.

    **Good bio formula:** [What you do] + [Who it's for] + [What makes you different]

    **Example:** "Atlanta-based DJ and producer. I drop exclusive sets, behind-the-scenes studio content, and live sessions every week. Hit subscribe for the real stuff."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Website / Linktree" icon="link">
    Link to your primary hub or connect your Linktree to consolidate all your links in one place.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Category" icon="star" iconType="solid">
    **This is the most important field on the page.** Your category tells Fanbase's discovery system what kind of creator you are, which determines who sees your content in search and recommendations. Choose the one that most accurately describes your primary content.

    <Warning>Confirm whether Category is single-select or multi-select in the current app build.</Warning>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Birthday" icon="cake-candles">
    Required for age verification. Not displayed publicly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Revenue Profile and Payment Setup

At the bottom of your Edit Profile screen, you'll see a **Revenue Profile** section. If you haven't set up your payment account yet, you'll see a prompt:

> "Seems like your payment data is missing. Create a Payment Account."

- You don't have to complete this to publish content, but you should do it before you start earning.
- Loves and subscriptions can't be paid out until your payment profile is active.

### Profile Completion Checklist

Use this to make sure you're fully set up before your first post:

- [ ] Profile photo uploaded (clear, high-quality)
- [ ] Cover image uploaded
- [ ] Username chosen and confirmed
- [ ] Display name set
- [ ] Bio written (2-3 sentences, specific and compelling)
- [ ] Website or Linktree link added
- [ ] Category selected
- [ ] Birthday entered
- [ ] Payment account created (see Module 1.2)

### Bringing Your Audience With You

If you're already creating on TikTok or Instagram, Fanbase makes it easy to migrate your existing content so your profile isn't starting from zero.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap your Profile, then tap the Settings/gear icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Account section">
    Scroll down to the Account section in Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your import source">
    Choose "Import content from Instagram" or "Import content from TikTok."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the import">
    Follow the prompts to complete the one-time migration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  This is a one-time migration. Once completed, it cannot be repeated for the same account. Your original profile on TikTok or Instagram is not affected -- you're copying content, not moving it.
</Warning>
