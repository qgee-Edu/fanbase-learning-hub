## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Use all six content formats on Fanbase: Posts, Stories, Flickz, Fanbase Live, Audio Rooms, and Fanbase+
- Understand the difference between public and exclusive content
- Use the platform's engagement tools: Loves, GIFs, Reactions, and Comments
- Access and use new features introduced in Fanbase 1.5
- Migrate existing content from TikTok or Instagram

## Section 1 -- Content Formats

Fanbase gives you six ways to publish content. You don't have to use all of them, but understanding each one helps you show up for your audience in the right format at the right moment.

### Posts and Photos

Posts are the foundation of your Fanbase profile. They live permanently on your page (unless you delete them) and appear in your followers' feeds. Posts can include photos, videos up to 1 minute, and captions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the + button">
    Find it in the bottom center of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Post">
    Choose from the creation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your media">
    Select a photo or video from your camera roll, or shoot in-app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a caption">
    Be specific -- tell your audience what they're looking at and why it matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose visibility">
    Public (all users) or Exclusive (subscribers only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Post to publish">
    Your post is live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="What's New: Carousel Posts">
    Fanbase now supports Carousel posts -- share multiple photos or videos in a single swipeable post. Ideal for:

    - Before/after reveals
    - Step-by-step tutorials or behind-the-scenes series
    - Product showcases, lookbooks, or photo dumps

    <Tip>Lead with your strongest image. Fans decide whether to swipe based on the first frame.</Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="What's New: Zoom-In">
    Fans can now pinch-to-zoom on your photos and videos. This matters most for detail-heavy content: artwork, fitness form checks, product close-ups, and food photography.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Stories

Stories are time-limited posts that appear at the top of followers' feeds and disappear after 24 hours. Built for real-time connection: quick updates, behind-the-scenes moments, and fan interaction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap + then select Story" />
  <Step title="Capture or upload a photo or short video" />
  <Step title="Use the authoring tools to customize" />
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Public or Exclusive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Share Story to post" />
</Steps>

**Story Authoring Tools:**

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| GIFs and Stickers | Add animated GIFs and sticker overlays from the media library |
| Questions | Post a question for fans to respond to directly in Stories |
| Links | Embed a clickable URL in your Story |
| Text and Drawing | Overlay captions, handwriting, or callouts |

<Tip>
  **Audio Rooms in Stories:** If you're hosting a live Audio Room, it automatically appears in the Story bar for your followers. They can tap directly from the Story bar to join your room without navigating away.
</Tip>

### Flickz

Flickz is Fanbase's short-form video format -- think TikTok Reels or Instagram Shorts. Videos can be up to 2 minutes long and include built-in editing tools, filters, music, and effects.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap + then select Flickz" />
  <Step title="Record in-app or upload from your camera roll">
    Up to 2 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the editing suite">
    Trim, add music, apply filters or effects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a caption and add relevant tags" />
  <Step title="Post to your profile">
    Flickz also appear in the Discover feed for non-followers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Suggested Flickz now appear in the main feed, meaning your short videos can reach people who don't follow you yet. Flickz is one of the best formats for audience growth on Fanbase.
</Info>

### Fanbase Live

Fanbase Live lets you broadcast real-time video to your audience. Fans can watch, send Loves, leave comments, and interact with you during the stream.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap + then select Live" />
  <Step title="Add a title for your broadcast" />
  <Step title="Tap Go Live to start your stream" />
  <Step title="Interact with fans via live chat and Loves" />
  <Step title="End the stream when done">
    You'll have the option to save the replay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="What's New: Multiperson Live">
    You can now go live with multiple people at the same time. This opens up:

    - Co-hosted shows and podcasts
    - Interviews and creator collabs
    - Group Q&As, panel discussions, or community events

    To invite someone: tap the invite icon during your broadcast and send an invite to another creator or fan.

  <Tab title="What's New: Watch Party">

    - Start from an eligible Fanbase+ video or a Vimeo/Dailymotion link
    - Fans join the room, the video plays in sync, and live chat runs alongside
    - Great for premieres, commentary sessions, or community viewing events
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Section 2 -- Public vs. Exclusive Content

Every piece of content you post is either **Public** or **Exclusive**. This determines who can see it and how fans access it.

| | Public | Exclusive |
|---|---|---|
| **Who can see it** | Anyone on Fanbase | Only fans who subscribe to your page |
| **Does it earn?** | Indirectly -- attracts followers who may subscribe | Yes -- unlocks when a fan subscribes |
| **Best used for** | Discovery content, teasers, announcements | Premium content, behind-the-scenes, subscriber perks |
| **Paywall?** | No, free to view | Subscription required. No individual unlock. |

<Warning>
  **PLATFORM UPDATE:** Fanbase removed individual content paywalls. Fans must subscribe to see exclusive content -- they can no longer unlock a single post with Loves.

  **Public content = growth. Exclusive content = subscriber value.**
</Warning>

## Section 3 -- Engagement Tools

### Loves (Virtual Tips)

Loves are Fanbase's virtual currency. Fans purchase Love packs and send them to you as tips on posts, in live streams, during Audio Rooms, or as a reaction to any content.

| Love Pack | Value |
|---|---|
| 100 Loves | $0.50 |
| 500 Loves | $2.50 |
| 1,000 Loves | $5.00 |
| 2,500 Loves | $12.50 |
| 10,000 Loves | $50.00 |
| 25,000 Loves | $125.00 |

_1 Love = $0.005 (half a penny). Fans tap the heart to send Love, or hold down to send a larger amount at once._

**What's New: Big Love**

Big Love is an enhanced tipping action that lets fans send a dramatically larger burst of Loves in one gesture. Designed for peak moments: live streams, special announcements, or when a fan really wants to show up for you.

### Comments and GIFs

Fans can comment on any public or exclusive post. Comments now support:

- **GIFs in Comments:** fans and creators can reply with animated GIFs directly in the comment thread
- **GIF Reactions:** fans can react to content with a GIF instead of just a text emoji

### Subscriptions

Subscriptions are the primary monetization mechanism on Fanbase. Fans pay a monthly fee to subscribe to your page and get access to all your exclusive content.

- You set your own subscription price: **$2.99 to $99.99/month**
- **Subscription Price Selection** lets you offer multiple price points for fans to choose from
- Subscribers see all content marked Exclusive, past and future

## Section 4 -- Bringing Your Content to Fanbase

If you're already creating on TikTok or Instagram, you can migrate your existing content to Fanbase in one step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap your Profile icon (bottom right)" />
  <Step title="Tap the Settings/gear icon (top right of your profile page)" />
  <Step title="Scroll to the Account section" />
  <Step title="Select your import source">
    Choose "Import content from TikTok" or "Import content from Instagram."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the prompts to authorize the import" />
</Steps>

- This is a **one-time migration.** Once completed, it cannot be repeated for the same account.
- Your original profile is not affected. You're copying content, not moving it.
- You can also add a YouTube video URL as exclusive content -- useful for repurposing long-form content.

## Section 5 -- Platform Experience Updates

### Dark Mode

Fanbase now supports full dark mode. Toggle it on via **Settings > Appearance > Dark Mode.** Applies across the entire app.

### Profile Spotlight Redesign

The Profile Spotlight section has been redesigned with a more compact layout, more white space, and tabs for: Posts, Videos, Audio, Podcast (if applicable), and Favorites.

Explore page Spotlight content is now ordered by: verified status, subscriber count, likes, then followers.

### Direct Messages

- Threads now open at the newest message automatically
- Long press on a message shows the timestamp and enables copy
- DM previews adapt correctly to light and dark mode
