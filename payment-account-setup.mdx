## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Locate the payment setup prompt in the Fanbase app
- Connect a PayPal account to receive earnings
- Understand what information is required and why
- Know how to manage your account and cash out

---

<Warning>
  You can post content and earn Loves and subscriptions without a payment account, but **you cannot cash out your earnings until your payment account is fully set up.** Do this early. Don't leave money sitting in your balance.
</Warning>

## Lesson 1 -- Where to Find Payment Setup

There are two ways to access the payment account setup:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Option A -- From Your Profile (Recommended)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Tap your Profile icon">
        Bottom right of the nav bar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap the Settings/gear icon">
        Top right of your profile page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Edit Profile" />
      <Step title="Scroll to the bottom of the Edit Profile screen" />
      <Step title="Find the Revenue Profile section">
        You'll see the prompt: "Seems like your payment data is missing. Create a Payment Account."
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Create Payment Account to begin" />
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Option B -- From Settings">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your Profile, then go to Settings" />
      <Step title="Scroll to the Payment or Revenue section" />
      <Step title="Tap Payment Account or Connect PayPal" />
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Lesson 2 -- Setting Up Your Account

Fanbase uses **PayPal** as the payout method. You'll need an active PayPal account to complete setup.

<Info>
  Don't have a PayPal account yet? Create a free one at [paypal.com](https://paypal.com) before continuing.
</Info>

**What you'll need:**

- An active PayPal account (personal or business)
- The email address linked to your PayPal account
- Your legal name and date of birth (required for identity/tax verification)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Create Payment Account">
    From your Edit Profile screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your legal first and last name">
    Must match your PayPal account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your date of birth">
    Required for identity and tax verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your PayPal email address" />
  <Step title="Review and confirm your information" />
  <Step title="Tap Save / Connect">
    Your payment account is now active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Your personal information is used for payment and tax purposes only. It is not displayed publicly on your Fanbase profile.
</Info>

## Lesson 3 -- Managing Your Payment Account

Once your payment account is set up, you can manage it from the same Settings area.

**What you can do:**

- **Update PayPal email:** if you change your PayPal account, update it here to avoid missed payouts
- **View current balance:** accessible from your Revenue Dashboard (see Module 1.1)
- **Request a cashout:** once your balance reaches $5.00, tap Cash Out to initiate a transfer to PayPal

### How to Cash Out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to your Revenue Dashboard" />
  <Step title="Confirm your balance is at least $5.00" />
  <Step title="Tap Cash Out" />
  <Step title="Confirm the PayPal account on file" />
  <Step title="Submit">
    Funds will transfer to your PayPal account within the standard processing window.
  </Step>
</Steps>
