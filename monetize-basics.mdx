## Lesson 1: The three monetization paths (and which one is yours)

Every creator on Fanbase ends up leaning into one of three primary paths. Understanding which one is yours determines what you post, how often, and how you talk to fans.

<Frame caption="Pick the monetization path that fits your content right now">
  <img src="/images/creator-academy/3-1-three-paths.svg" alt="The three monetization paths: Tip-Driven, Subscription-Driven, Hybrid" />
</Frame>

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Tip-Driven" icon="heart">
    **Loves do most of the work.** Best when you go live often, host Audio Rooms regularly, or your content has strong real-time moments fans want to celebrate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscription-Driven" icon="repeat">
    **Recurring subscribers do most of the work.** Best when you produce ongoing exclusive content that's worth committing to monthly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hybrid" icon="layer-group">
    **Loves and subscriptions both pull weight.** The most stable model. Public content drives discovery and tips; exclusive content drives subscriptions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Self-assessment: which path fits you right now?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Tip-Driven if..." icon="microphone">
    You go live multiple times a week. Your audience loves real-time interaction. You're a performer, musician, host, or improvisational creator. You don't have a steady stream of exclusive content (yet).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Subscription-Driven if..." icon="calendar">
    You publish on a predictable cadence. Your content has a clear ongoing 'value drip' (tutorials, behind-the-scenes, member-only series, courses, coaching). You'd rather build a smaller, paying community than chase volume.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Hybrid if..." icon="scale-balanced">
    You do both well. Your live energy is high *and* you have a steady stream of exclusive content. Most established creators end up here.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Pick your starting path and lean into it for 30 days. Don't try to do all three at once. You'll dilute your effort.
</Tip>

## Lesson 2: The follower-to-subscriber conversion funnel

Followers are free. Subscribers pay. The space between the two is where most creators lose money.

<Frame caption="The four-stage funnel: awareness, engagement, trust, conversion">
  <img src="/images/creator-academy/3-1-conversion-funnel.svg" alt="Follower to subscriber conversion funnel" />
</Frame>

Here's the funnel that actually converts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Awareness: public content does the discovery work">
    Public Posts and Flickz are how new people find you. This is your top of funnel. Don't gate this content. It exists to attract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engagement: Stories and comments build the habit">
    Stories appear daily. Comments build relationships. Loves tell you what's working. This stage is where a stranger becomes a regular.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trust: Live moments and DMs prove you're real">
    Going live or hosting Audio Rooms turns a regular into a fan. They see you in real time. They feel the relationship. Trust is what makes the subscribe button worth tapping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conversion: make the subscribe ask explicit">
    Don't assume fans know what they get when they subscribe. Tell them. Show them. Make the ask weekly, not occasionally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Where most creators leak fans

<Warning>
**Three common leaks:**
1. **No clear ask.** You assume the subscribe button is enough. It isn't. You need a verbal or written ask.
2. **Thin exclusive content.** Fans subscribe, see two posts, cancel. Make sure there's enough behind the wall.
3. **Treating public and exclusive the same.** If your public content is already premium, fans have no reason to subscribe.
</Warning>

### Three high-converting subscribe moments

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Mid-Live" icon="signal">
    During a live broadcast: 'If you want the full version of this, it's on my exclusive page. Tap subscribe.'
  </Card>
  <Card title="End of a public Flickz" icon="film">
    Drop a line of text at the end: *'Full version on my page. Subscribe to see it all.'*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Story tease" icon="circle-play">
    Post a teaser Story showing the cover of an exclusive piece, with a subscribe sticker.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Lesson 3: Cross-platform growth (bring your audience with you)

Fanbase doesn't replace your other platforms. It earns you money from the audience you've already built. Cross-platform growth is the engine.

<Frame caption="Discovery happens elsewhere. Revenue happens on Fanbase.">
  <img src="/images/creator-academy/3-1-cross-platform-flow.svg" alt="Cross-platform discovery feeds Fanbase as the monetization hub" />
</Frame>

### The two-platform rule

Most creators don't need to be on six platforms. They need to be on two: one for discovery, one for monetization.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Discovery platform" icon="magnifying-glass">
    **TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube.** This is where new people find you. Optimize for reach.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Monetization platform" icon="dollar-sign">
    **Fanbase.** This is where your audience converts to revenue. Optimize for depth.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### What to actually post on your discovery platform

Three rules:

1. **Don't repost Fanbase exclusive content elsewhere.** That defeats the whole point of exclusive.
2. **Do tease your Fanbase exclusive content.** A 10-second preview of a 5-minute exclusive piece is great cross-platform fuel.
3. **Always show the destination.** Username, link in bio, on-screen graphic. Make it obvious where the full version lives.

### Migrating your existing audience

If you're starting on Fanbase but you've already built on TikTok or Instagram, run the migration described in [Module 2.2](/knowledge-center/fanbase-features#section-4-bringing-your-content-to-fanbase). It seeds your profile and gives new visitors something to scroll through immediately.

<Tip>
**Day-one move:** Run the migration, then post one public Flickz teasing your first exclusive piece. New visitors will find a profile that already feels active, not empty.
</Tip>

## Lesson 4: Your first 30 days, in plain order

<Frame caption="A 4-week plan: foundation, activation, amplification, conversion">
  <img src="/images/creator-academy/3-1-30-day-plan.svg" alt="30-day monetization plan timeline" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Week 1: Foundation">
    Complete [Module 2.1 profile setup](/knowledge-center/getting-started). Complete [Module 1.2 payment account setup](/business-center/payment-account-setup). Set your subscription price (see [Module 1.3](/business-center/subscriptions-and-pricing)). Migrate content from TikTok or Instagram.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week 2: Activation">
    Post one public piece per day. Drop two exclusive pieces this week. Go live once. Make one explicit subscribe ask during the live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week 3: Amplification">
    Tease one piece of exclusive content on your discovery platform. Host one Audio Room. Reply to every comment on Fanbase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week 4: Conversion">
    Review your Revenue Dashboard. Look at your Loves-to-Subscriptions ratio. If Loves are high and subscriptions are low, your subscribe ask needs to get louder.
  </Step>
</Steps>
