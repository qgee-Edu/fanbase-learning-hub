## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Locate the payment setup prompt inside the Fanbase app
- Connect a payout account to receive earnings
- Understand what information is required and why
- Know what to do if you hit an error

<Warning>
**Why do this first?** You can post content and earn Loves and subscriptions without a payment account, but you cannot cash out your earnings until your payment account is fully set up. Do this early. Don't leave money sitting in your balance.
</Warning>

## Lesson 1: Where to find Payment Setup

There are two ways to access payment account setup. Use whichever path matches where you are in the app.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From your Profile (recommended)">
    <Frame caption="Profile screen with Settings icon highlighted in the top right corner">
      <img src="/images/business-center/1-2-profile-settings-icon.png" alt="Profile screen with Settings icon highlighted" />
    </Frame>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your Profile">
        Tap your **Profile** icon (bottom right of the nav bar).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Settings">
        Tap the **Settings/gear** icon (top right of your profile page).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Edit Profile">
        From the Settings menu, tap **Edit Profile**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Scroll to the bottom">
        Scroll all the way down on the Edit Profile screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find the Revenue Profile prompt">
        You'll see the Revenue Profile section with a prompt: *'Seems like your payment data is missing. Create Payment Account.'*
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start setup">
        Tap **Create Payment Account** to begin.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From Settings directly">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings">
        Go to **Profile > Settings** and scroll to the Payment or Revenue section.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Payment Account">
        Tap **Payment Account** or **Connect Stripe**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Lesson 2: Setting up your account

Fanbase uses Stripe as the payout method. You'll need an active Stripe account to complete setup. If you don't have one, create a free Stripe account at [stripe.com](https://stripe.com) before continuing.

### What you'll need

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Stripe account" icon="building-columns">
    An active Stripe account (personal or business).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stripe email" icon="envelope">
    The email address linked to your Stripe account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ID info" icon="id-card">
    Your legal name and date of birth, used for tax compliance.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Setup steps

<Frame caption="Annotated 'Create Payment Account' form with fields labeled: Legal Name, Date of Birth, Stripe Email">
  <img src="/images/business-center/1-2-setup-form.png" alt="Annotated Create Payment Account form" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the flow">
    Tap **Create Payment Account** from your Edit Profile screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your legal name">
    Enter your legal first and last name. These must match your Stripe account exactly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your date of birth">
    Required for identity and tax verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Stripe email">
    Use the email address linked to your Stripe account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Double-check that the legal name and email match what's on your Stripe account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save / Connect">
    Tap **Save** or **Connect**. Your payment account is now active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Your personal information is used for payment and tax purposes only. It is not displayed publicly on your Fanbase profile.
</Info>

## Lesson 3: Managing your payment account

Once your payment account is set up, you can manage it from the same Settings area.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Update your Stripe email" icon="pen-to-square">
    If you change your Stripe account, update the email here to avoid missed payouts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="View your current balance" icon="wallet">
    Accessible from your Revenue Dashboard. See [Module 1.1](/business-center/getting-paid).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Request a cashout" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    Once your balance reaches $5.00, tap **Cash Out** to initiate transfer to Stripe.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Cashout process

<Frame caption="Cashout confirmation screen showing balance, connected account, and a 'Cash Out' confirmation button">
  <img src="/images/business-center/1-2-cashout-screen.png" alt="Cashout confirmation screen" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Revenue Dashboard">
    Navigate to your Revenue Dashboard from Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your balance">
    Your balance must be at least $5.00.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Cash Out">
    Tap the **Cash Out** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Stripe account on file">
    Make sure the connected account is correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Funds will transfer to your Stripe account within the standard processing window.
  </Step>
</Steps>
