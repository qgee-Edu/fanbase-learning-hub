## What you'll learn in this hub

The Business Center is built for one thing: making sure you understand the money side of Fanbase from day one. No guessing. No surprises. No money sitting in your balance because you skipped a step.

By the end of these three modules, you'll know exactly how earnings work on Fanbase, how to set up the account that lets you cash out, and how to price your subscription so fans actually subscribe.
