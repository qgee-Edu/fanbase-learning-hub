## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Use all six content formats on Fanbase: Posts, Stories, Flickz, Fanbase Live, Audio Rooms, and Fanbase+
- Understand the difference between Public and Exclusive content
- Use the platform's engagement tools: Loves, GIFs, Reactions, and Comments
- Access and use new features introduced in Fanbase 1.5
- Migrate existing content from TikTok or Instagram

## Section 1: Content formats

Fanbase gives you six ways to publish content. You don't have to use all of them, but understanding each one helps you show up for your audience in the right format at the right moment.

### 1.1 Posts and Photos

Posts are the foundation of your Fanbase profile. They live permanently on your page (unless you delete them) and appear in your followers' feeds. Posts can include photos, videos up to 1 minute, and captions.

<Frame caption="Annotated post composer with media picker, caption field, and Public vs Exclusive visibility toggle">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-post-composer.png" alt="Post composer screen" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the creation menu">
    Tap the **+** button in the bottom center of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Post">
    Choose **Post** from the creation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your media">
    Choose a photo or video from your camera roll, or shoot in-app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a strong caption">
    Be specific. Tell your audience what they're looking at and why it matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Choose **Public** (all users) or **Exclusive** (subscribers only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Post">
    Publish to your profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### What's new: Carousel posts

Fanbase now supports Carousel posts. You can share multiple photos or videos in a single swipeable post. Ideal for before/after reveals, step-by-step tutorials, product showcases, and photo dumps.

<Tip>
**Carousel tip:** Lead with your strongest image. Fans decide whether to swipe based on the first frame. Put your hook up front.
</Tip>

#### What's new: Zoom-in

Fans can now pinch-to-zoom on your photos and videos. This matters most for detail-heavy content: artwork, fitness form checks, product close-ups, and food photography.

### 1.2 Stories

Stories are time-limited posts that appear at the top of followers' feeds and disappear after 24 hours. Built for real-time connection: quick updates, behind-the-scenes moments, polls, and fan interaction.

<Frame caption="Story authoring screen showing GIFs, Stickers, Questions, Links, and Text tools in the side toolbar">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-story-tools.png" alt="Story authoring tools" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create menu">
    Tap **+** then select **Story**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture or upload">
    Take a photo or short video, or upload one from your camera roll.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the authoring tools">
    Add GIFs, stickers, text, drawings, or interactive elements. (See the toolset below.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Public or Exclusive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Share Story">
    Your Story is now live for 24 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### Story authoring tools

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="GIFs and Stickers" icon="face-laugh">
    Add animated GIFs and sticker overlays from the media library.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Questions" icon="circle-question">
    Post a question for fans to respond to directly in Stories.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Links" icon="link">
    Embed a clickable URL in your Story (great for promotions and new content).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Text and Drawing" icon="pen">
    Overlay captions, handwriting, or callouts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

#### Audio Rooms in Stories

If you're hosting a live Audio Room, it automatically appears in the Story bar for your followers. They can tap directly from the Story bar to join your room without navigating away. This significantly increases discovery for live audio sessions.

### 1.3 Flickz

Flickz is Fanbase's short-form video format. Think of it as the platform's version of TikTok Reels or Instagram Shorts. Videos can be up to 2 minutes long and include built-in editing tools, filters, music, and effects.

<Frame caption="Flickz editing suite with trim controls, music picker, and filter options visible">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-flickz-editor.png" alt="Flickz editing suite" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create menu">
    Tap **+** then select **Flickz**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record or upload">
    Record in-app (up to 2 minutes) or upload from your camera roll.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your video">
    Use the editing suite: trim, add music, apply filters or effects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a caption and tags">
    Add a strong caption and relevant tags so the right audience finds it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post">
    Flickz appear on your profile and in the Discover feed for non-followers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Suggested Flickz now appear in the main feed, meaning your short videos can reach people who don't follow you yet. This makes Flickz one of the best formats for audience growth on Fanbase.
</Tip>

### 1.4 Fanbase Live

Fanbase Live lets you broadcast real-time video to your audience. Fans can watch, send Loves, leave comments, and interact with you during the stream. Live is one of the highest-engagement formats on the platform.

<Frame caption="An active Fanbase Live broadcast: viewer count, live timer, fan chat with Loves and comments, and the gift/Love send buttons at the bottom">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-live-setup.png" alt="Active Fanbase Live broadcast with live chat" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create menu">
    Tap **+** then select **Live**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a title">
    Give your broadcast a compelling title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go Live">
    Tap **Go Live** to start streaming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interact with fans">
    Respond to live chat and Loves in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the stream">
    When done, end the broadcast. You'll have the option to save the replay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### What's new: Multiperson Live

You can now go live with multiple people at the same time. This opens up co-hosted shows, interviews, group Q&As, and community events.

To invite someone: tap the invite icon during your broadcast and send an invite to another creator or fan.

#### What's new: Watch Party

Watch Party lets you watch Fanbase+ content (as well as Vimeo and Dailymotion videos) together with your fans in real time. You control playback. Fans watch in sync with you.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Start it" icon="play">
    Launch from an eligible FB+ video or a Vimeo/Dailymotion link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fans join" icon="users">
    The video plays in sync and live chat runs alongside.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use it for" icon="star">
    Premieres, commentary sessions, or community viewing events.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### 1.5 Audio Rooms and Camps

Audio Rooms are live audio sessions you host for your audience. Think of them as a live podcast, a community radio show, or a live Q&A. Fans can listen, react with Loves, and participate as speakers if you invite them. **Camps** are interest-based communities where Audio Rooms often live.

<Frame caption="A live Audio Room hosted in the Music Business Camp, showing Host and Speaker grid, listener count, and Love/Raise Hand/Share/Mute controls">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-audio-room.png" alt="Active Fanbase Audio Room with speakers and listeners" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create menu">
    Tap **+** then select **Audio**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Title your room">
    Set a clear title and topic so the right listeners join.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the room">
    Tap **Start Room** to go live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite speakers">
    Invite specific fans, or let listeners request to speak.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the room">
    When done, end the session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### Audio Room enhancements (Fanbase 1.5)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Audio Transcription" icon="closed-captioning">
    Live transcription runs during your room, making sessions accessible and searchable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Story Bar integration" icon="circle-play">
    Your active Audio Room appears in the Story bar automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Eavesdrop via Stories" icon="headphones">
    Fans can listen to a live Audio Room directly from a Story preview without joining as a participant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Moderator controls" icon="user-shield">
    Creators can silence speakers. Regular moderators can no longer demote creator-level moderators.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Branded Audio Rooms" icon="briefcase">
    Available for creators with branded content integrations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Fanbase+ is the platform's long-form video channel, similar to YouTube but built for creator monetization from day one. You can upload high-quality, full-length video from your phone or desktop. FB+ received a redesign in Fanbase 1.5.

<Frame caption="Fanbase+ upload screen with file picker, title, description, thumbnail, and visibility toggle">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-fb-plus-upload.png" alt="Fanbase+ upload screen" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create menu">
    Tap **+** then select **Plus (FB+)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Upload your video from your phone or desktop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add metadata">
    Add a title, description, and thumbnail.

## Section 2: Public vs. Exclusive content

Every piece of content you post is either Public or Exclusive. This determines who can see it and how fans access it.

<Frame caption="Side-by-side comparison: a Public post (open eye icon, 'visible to all') versus an Exclusive post (lock icon, 'subscribers only')">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-public-vs-exclusive.png" alt="Public vs Exclusive content comparison" />
</Frame>

| | **Public** | **Exclusive** |
|---|---|---|
| **Who can see it** | Anyone on Fanbase (and off-platform if shared) | Only fans who subscribe to your page |
| **Does it earn?** | Indirectly. Attracts followers who may subscribe. | Yes. Unlocks when a fan subscribes. |
| **Best used for** | Discovery content, teasers, announcements, free value | Premium content, behind-the-scenes, subscriber perks |
| **Paywall?** | No, free to view | Subscription required. No individual unlock. |

<Info>
**Platform update:** Fanbase removed individual content paywalls. Fans must subscribe to see exclusive content. They can no longer unlock a single post with Loves.

**Strategy in one line:** Public content = growth. Exclusive content = subscriber value.
</Info>

<Warning>
**Video note (Video 2):** Existing monetization overview shows the old paywall/individual unlock model. Requires a full rewrite to reflect the subscription-only exclusive content model.
</Warning>

## Section 3: Engagement tools

Engagement is how fans interact with your content and how you build the kind of relationship that converts followers into paying subscribers.

### 3.1 Loves (Virtual Tips)

Loves are Fanbase's virtual currency. Fans purchase Love packs and send them to creators as tips on posts, in live streams, during Audio Rooms, or as a reaction to any content. Loves convert directly to real earnings in your Revenue Dashboard.

<Frame caption="Love pack sizes (100, 500, 1,000, 2,500, 10,000, 25,000) shown as purchase options in the Fanbase wallet">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-love-packs.png" alt="Love pack sizes" />
</Frame>

- **Pack sizes available to fans:** 100, 500, 1,000, 2,500, 10,000, and 25,000 Loves
- **Rate:** 1 Love = $0.005 (half a penny)
- **How fans tip:** Tap the heart on any post to send Love. Hold down to send a larger amount at once.

#### What's new: Big Love

Big Love is an enhanced tipping action that lets fans send a dramatically larger burst of Loves in one gesture. Designed for peak moments: live streams, special announcements, or when a fan really wants to show up for you.

### 3.2 Comments and GIFs

Fans can comment on any public or exclusive post. Comments now support:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="GIFs in Comments" icon="film">
    Fans and creators can reply with animated GIFs directly in the comment thread.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GIF Reactions" icon="face-smile">
    Fans can react to content with a GIF instead of just a text emoji.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### 3.3 Subscriptions

Subscriptions are the primary monetization mechanism on Fanbase. Fans pay a monthly fee to subscribe to your page and get access to all your exclusive content.

- You set your own subscription price from Fanbase's tiers ($0.99 to $49.99/month). See [Module 1.3](/business-center/subscriptions-and-pricing) for the full pricing strategy guide.
- **Subscription Price Selection** lets you offer multiple price points for fans to choose from
- Subscribers see all content marked Exclusive, past and future

<Warning>
**Verify:** Confirm the current subscription fee structure and whether Fanbase takes any platform percentage. Confirm how creators access Subscription Price Selection in-app.
</Warning>

### 3.4 Live Gaming: Trivia

Fanbase now supports live in-app Trivia as an interactive content format. Creators can host Trivia games with their audience in real time. Fans participate directly within the app.

<Warning>
**Verify:** Confirm how Trivia is hosted. Whether it's creator-initiated via the Live flow or a separate content type. Confirm if Trivia is available to all creators or a specific tier.

## Section 4: Bringing your content to Fanbase

If you're already creating on TikTok or Instagram, you can migrate your existing content to Fanbase in one step. This gives your profile instant depth.

<Frame caption="Settings > Account screen with 'Import from TikTok' and 'Import from Instagram' options highlighted">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-content-migration.png" alt="Content migration screen" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile">
    Tap your Profile icon (bottom right).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap the Settings/gear icon (top right of your profile page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Account section">
    Scroll to the Account section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your import source">
    Select **Import content from TikTok** or **Import content from Instagram**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Follow the prompts to authorize the import.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
- This is a one-time migration. Once completed, it cannot be repeated for the same account.
- Your original profile is not affected. You're copying content, not moving it.
- You can also add a YouTube video URL as exclusive content. Useful for repurposing long-form content.
</Info>

<Warning>
**Video note (Video 6):** Import walkthrough is largely accurate but missing a few navigation steps in current UI. Verify current path, especially whether Settings is accessed via gear icon or a different route.
</Warning>

## Section 5: Platform experience updates

Fanbase 1.5 introduced several quality-of-life improvements that affect how creators show up on the platform.

<Frame caption="Three side-by-side mockups: Dark Mode toggled on, redesigned Profile Spotlight with new tab layout, and updated DM thread view">
  <img src="/images/knowledge-center/2-2-platform-updates.png" alt="Platform experience updates" />
</Frame>

### 5.1 Dark Mode

Fanbase now supports full dark mode. Toggle it on via **Settings > Appearance > Dark Mode**. Applies across the entire app.

### 5.2 Profile Spotlight redesign

The Profile Spotlight section has been redesigned. The new layout is more compact, with more white space, and includes tabs for: **Posts, Videos, Audio, Podcast (if applicable),** and **Favorites**.

- Swiping the Spotlight section opens the story/update sheet
- Explore page Spotlight content is now ordered by: verified status, subscriber count, likes, then followers

### 5.3 Direct Messages

- Threads now open at the newest message automatically
- Long press on a message shows the timestamp and enables copy
- DM previews adapt correctly to light and dark mode
