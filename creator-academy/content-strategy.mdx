## Lesson 1: What goes where (a format-by-format strategy)

Every Fanbase format does a specific job. Posting the wrong content type in the wrong format is the single most common reason creators feel like they're 'working hard for no reason.'

<Frame caption="A 6-column grid mapping each format to its primary job: Post=permanent value, Story=daily connection, Flickz=growth, Live=relationship, Audio=community, FB+=premium depth">
  <img src="/images/creator-academy/3-2-format-jobs.png" alt="Job of each Fanbase format" />
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Posts: permanent value" icon="image">
    Use for: announcements, signature work, evergreen content, important moments. Posts live on your profile forever, so think of them as the *body of work* a new visitor scrolls through.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stories: daily connection" icon="circle-play">
    Use for: behind-the-scenes, polls, questions, quick updates, in-the-moment thoughts. Stories disappear after 24 hours, so they're low-pressure and high-frequency. The goal is *to be seen daily*.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Flickz: growth" icon="film">
    Use for: short, punchy, scroll-stopping content designed to get the algorithm to put you in front of non-followers. Flickz are your discovery engine.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fanbase Live: relationship" icon="signal">
    Use for: real-time interaction, deeper conversations, premieres, Q&A. Live is where fans become real fans because they get to be in the same room as you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Audio Rooms: community" icon="microphone">
    Use for: ongoing community discussions, niche conversations, recurring shows. Audio Rooms are where a community forms around you, not just an audience.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fanbase+ (FB+): premium depth" icon="video">
    Use for: long-form, structured, high-production content. Tutorials, series episodes, deep-dives. FB+ is what makes a subscription feel worth it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Lesson 2: The weekly content rhythm

You don't need to post every format every day. You need a rhythm fans can predict.

Here's a sustainable starter rhythm. Adapt it to your life, but don't drop below this floor if you're trying to grow.

<Frame caption="Weekly content rhythm calendar showing 1 Flickz per day, 1 Post per week, 3 Stories per day, 1 Live per week, 1 Audio Room every 2 weeks, 1 FB+ video per month">
  <img src="/images/creator-academy/3-2-weekly-rhythm.png" alt="Weekly content rhythm" />
</Frame>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Daily" icon="sun">
    1 Flickz (growth)
    3 Stories (connection)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weekly" icon="calendar-week">
    1 signature Post (permanent value)
    1 Live broadcast (relationship + subscribe ask)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Every 2 weeks" icon="calendar-day">
    1 Audio Room (community)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Monthly" icon="calendar">
    1 Fanbase+ video (premium depth)
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
**Sustainability rule:** If you can't keep up this rhythm for 4 weeks straight, cut it in half. Consistency beats volume. A creator posting 3 Stories a day for 90 days will outperform a creator who posts 10 things in week one and disappears.
</Tip>

### Public vs. Exclusive split

A rough starting split for a Hybrid creator:

- **70% public content:** Drives discovery. Builds your top of funnel.
- **30% exclusive content:** Rewards the subscription. Makes paying feel worth it.

If your subscription numbers are flat after 30 days, shift the ratio: more exclusive, more 'subscribe to see the rest' framing on public pieces.

## Lesson 3: Using Stories to build fan relationships

Stories are the most under-used high-leverage format on Fanbase. They're low-effort, disappear in 24 hours, and they're where fans feel closest to you.

<Frame caption="A four-Story sequence showing the daily Story formula: 'Wake up' (morning context) > 'Working on' (mid-day) > 'Question for fans' (afternoon) > 'Wrap up + tease' (evening)">
  <img src="/images/creator-academy/3-2-daily-story-sequence.png" alt="Daily Story sequence" />
</Frame>

### The daily Story formula

A simple 4-Story rhythm that works for almost any creator:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Morning: context">
    A short Story setting up your day. *'Heading into the studio, dropping a new track tonight.'*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mid-day: behind-the-scenes">
    A photo or short clip of what you're actually working on. Show the work, not just the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Afternoon: interaction">
    Post a Question, Poll, or Sticker prompt. This is where fans get to participate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evening: wrap up + tease">
    Recap the day and tease tomorrow. *'Long day. New exclusive piece dropping at 9pm if you're subscribed.'*
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Use Story tools intentionally

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Questions sticker" icon="circle-question">
    Best for: getting content ideas straight from fans, building a Q&A archive, increasing reply rate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Polls" icon="square-check">
    Best for: low-pressure interaction, content direction (*'Which one should I drop next?'*), warming up new followers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GIFs and stickers" icon="face-laugh">
    Best for: humor, personality, lightening heavier content, making a Story feel less polished and more human.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Links" icon="link">
    Best for: pointing to your latest exclusive content, off-platform launches, or your full profile.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### One thing to never do with Stories

Don't only post your published content. Stories are for the *space between* the polished content. If every Story is a 'Go check out my new post' graphic, fans will start skipping.

## Lesson 4: Going live with intention

Going live is the highest-leverage activity on Fanbase. It's also where the most creators freeze up because they don't have a plan.

<Frame caption="Live broadcast structure shown as a 5-phase timeline: Pre-Live setup, Open the room, Deliver value, Make the ask, Close strong">
  <img src="/images/creator-academy/3-2-live-structure.png" alt="Live broadcast structure" />
</Frame>

### A repeatable live structure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pre-Live: tease and announce">
    Post a Story 1 to 2 hours before. Let fans know the topic and the start time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the room (first 5 minutes)">
    Greet people by name as they join. Re-state the topic. Tell fans how they can participate (chat, send Loves, request to speak in an Audio Room).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver value (middle 70%)">
    Whatever you came on to do: teach, perform, discuss, react. The goal is for someone watching to feel like they got something out of the time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the subscribe ask">
    Once mid-broadcast. Once near the end. Be specific: *'If this is useful, my exclusive page has the full version of [thing]. Tap subscribe.'*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close strong">
    Thank fans by name. Tease what's next. End the broadcast cleanly so the post-live stats and replay save properly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### When to use Live vs. Audio Rooms vs. Watch Party

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Fanbase Live" icon="video">
    **Use when** you want fans to see you. Performance, demos, reaction content, big announcements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio Room" icon="microphone">
    **Use when** the conversation matters more than the visual. Q&As, community discussions, recurring shows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Watch Party" icon="film">
    **Use when** you want to share an experience around content. Premieres, commentary sessions, community viewings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
**Verify:** Confirm whether post-live stats include a 'subscribed during live' metric. If yes, that's the single most important number for tuning your subscribe ask.
</Warning>

## Lesson 5: A content audit you can run anytime

Every 30 days, run this audit on your Fanbase profile.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Discovery audit" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Open your last 10 Flickz. Which 2 got the most reach? Make more like those.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Engagement audit" icon="comments">
    Open your Notifications. Which post got the most comments and Loves? That's your audience signaling what they want more of.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Conversion audit" icon="user-plus">
    Open your Revenue Dashboard. How many new subscribers did you gain this month? When did they subscribe (mid-live? after a teaser?). Make more of *that* moment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cancellation audit" icon="user-minus">
    Subscribers who cancel are the loudest feedback you'll get. Look at when they cancelled relative to your exclusive content drops. If they cancel after one month, the wall isn't deep enough yet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
